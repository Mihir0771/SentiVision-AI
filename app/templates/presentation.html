🧠 SentiVision AI

Multi-Modal Real-Time Sentiment & Facial Emotion Recognition Platform

Unifying deep learning Natural Language Processing with real-time Computer Vision to decode emotional intelligence across written text, tabular datasets, and live webcam video streams.

Author / Presenter: Mihir (@Mihir0771)
Stack: Python • Flask • OpenCV • Transformers • MongoDB

01. Problem & Vision

Bridging the Multimodal Emotional Gap

Single-Modality Blindspots

Traditional sentiment tools only inspect text or reviews, completely missing visual expressions, micro-reactions, and live customer feedback during digital interactions.

Single-Model Bias

Relying on a single AI model produces skewed sentiment scores. Deep transformers excel at nuances, while lexicon models excel at social idioms and emojis.

High Friction Barriers

Most tools gate features behind compulsory logins, credit cards, or rate-limited paid tiers. SentiVision AI was engineered as a frictionless open-source system for everyone.

03. Computer Vision Engine

Hybrid Neural & Landmark Emotion Detection

YuNet Face Detection & CLAHE
  • OpenCV YuNet ONNX: State-of-the-art fast neural detector locating faces and 5 facial landmark keypoints in under 15ms.
  • Adaptive CLAHE: Contrast Limited Adaptive Histogram Equalization eliminates lighting variations (shadows, backlight, low contrast).
  • Dynamic Face Padding: 10% context expansion to ensure brow and chin features are preserved for emotion recognition.
FERPlus & Landmark Geometric Fusion
  • 7 Universal Emotions: Quantifies Happy, Sad, Angry, Surprise, Fear, Disgust, and Neutral.
  • Geometric Prior Calibration: Uses mouth-width-to-eye-distance ratios to detect smiles and mouth opening ratios to detect surprise without sticking to neutral.
  • Sub-40ms Inference: Executes at 24+ FPS on standard CPU without requiring heavy GPU clusters.

04. Natural Language Processing

Multi-Model Weighted Consensus Ensemble

Weight: 50%
CardiffNLP Twitter-RoBERTa

Deep transformer neural model trained on 124M+ tweets for deep semantic context and sarcasm awareness.

Weight: 30%
VADER Lexicon Analyzer

Rule-based engine tuned specifically for social media jargon, capitalization intensity, and emoji sentiment.

Weight: 20%
TextBlob Polarity

Syntactic lexicon model providing complementary objectivity vs subjectivity validation.

05. Platform Capabilities

Batch Processing, Scraping & Analytics

Tabular Ingestion

Upload CSV, JSON, and Excel review dumps. The auto-column detection automatically locates review fields and exports scored tables.

DOM Web Scraper

Paste any blog post, article, or review thread URL to scrape text bodies and analyze consensus opinion on the fly.

Open REST API v1

Clean developer API endpoints for external microservices to submit text or base64 frames for real-time scoring.
